# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project spaces should remain respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.
Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and behavior without personal attacks;
- provide actionable feedback;
- respect different experience levels and backgrounds;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and unwanted personal attention;
- protect private information and security reports;
- accept maintainer decisions made to protect the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, threats, and
retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard. Repeated or serious violations may
result in a temporary or permanent ban.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be
reviewed without public disclosure where possible.

## Scope

This code applies to repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers,
project chat, events, and public representation of the project.
